🌄 Where it sits on the flyer

The Eagle opens page two of the flyer, The Meeting of the Eagle and the Condor, right after the page’s opening words:

Plants & Miracles brings together the wisdom of plant medicine and the miracle-minded path of “A Course in Miracles”. One stream descends through the heart of Christian mysticism; the other rises through the living intelligence of sacred plants. Together they open a pathway of healing, forgiveness, illumination, and oneness.

Two more passages follow it on the same page: 🐍 The Condor and 💛 Their Union.

🌿 The sky-blue strand

Across the foundation’s sites, the Eagle is drawn as the sky-blue strand of the vine: the strand for A Course in Miracles. It twines upward with the Condor’s gold strand until the two meet in a single point of light.
