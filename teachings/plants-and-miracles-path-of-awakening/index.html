✨ The Path of Awakening

The Ancient Caduceus: Release Kundalini & See the Golden Halo, by Awakening the Spiritual Eye of Light in the Spirit of Forgiveness

The Teaching of Caduceus describes the release of Kundalini up the spine & awakening of the Spiritual Eye of Light which reveals the Creations of Light.

As the centers are purified and brought into harmony with help of ayahuasca and the illumination vision the body becomes a vessel for peace instead of conflict.

The Golden Halo of Light symbolizes the Face of Christ, a vision of salvation through forgiveness and the remembrance of your true Self.

This journey culminates in the remembrance of angelic heritage, where Love, Harmony, and Beauty are revealed as your effortless natural state.

— Isaiah Kenney, Plants & Miracles flyer

The Path of Awakening is the flyer’s third page. The page before it points here: the Condor “invites purification and a teacher beyond the human condition to teach the Ancient Caduceus” (🐍 The Condor).

🪷 About the drawing

The flyer lays these centers along the spine of a seated, winged figure, each with a colored label; see the page as printed below. The drawing above keeps the flyer’s names, order, and label colors, and sets them on the foundation’s twin vine instead. The four passages keep the flyer’s layout too: two across the top, two across the bottom.
