🦅 Page two: The Meeting of the Eagle and the Condor

The Meeting of the Eagle and the Condor

Plants & Miracles – A Sufi Teaching of Love, Harmony, and Beauty

Plants & Miracles brings together the wisdom of plant medicine and the miracle-minded path of “A Course in Miracles”. One stream descends through the heart of Christian mysticism; the other rises through the living intelligence of sacred plants. Together they open a pathway of healing, forgiveness, illumination, and oneness.

The Eagle

The Eagle represents the spirit of Jesus and his an Christian Mysticism and A Course in Miracles. A teaching of miracle of forgiveness and illumination vision in remembrance of God.

The Condor

The Condor represents the plant medicine path & the spirit of Ayahuasca. It invites purification and a teacher beyond the human condition to teach the Ancient Caduceus.

Their Union

In Plants & Miracles, these two spirits meet in a universal teaching: love over fear and innocence over guilt. Together they teach you to fly again from the Heart of Silence.

“A journey into Love, Harmony, and Beauty in the remembrance of God.”

✨ Page three: The Path of Awakening

The Path of Awakening

The Ancient Caduceus: Release Kundalini & See the Golden Halo, by Awakening the Spiritual Eye of Light in the Spirit of Forgiveness

Labeled on the figure, top to bottom: Spiritual Eye of Light · Third-Eye Chakra · Throat Chakra · Heart Chakra · Solar Plexus Chakra · Navel Chakra · Sacral Chakra · Root Chakra

The Teaching of Caduceus describes the release of Kundalini up the spine & awakening of the Spiritual Eye of Light which reveals the Creations of Light.

As the centers are purified and brought into harmony with help of ayahuasca and the illumination vision the body becomes a vessel for peace instead of conflict.

The Golden Halo of Light symbolizes the Face of Christ, a vision of salvation through forgiveness and the remembrance of your true Self.

This journey culminates in the remembrance of angelic heritage, where Love, Harmony, and Beauty are revealed as your effortless natural state.

🧩 How these pages are made

  • 🪶 The flyer leads. Each page follows the flyer’s own headings and order: the cover, the Eagle, the Condor, their union, the Path of Awakening, and Kenney himself.
  • 💬 His words stay his. Anything set as a passage, or quoted above, is copied exactly as printed. The short notes around it only say where it sits on the flyer and how it connects to the rest of these sites; none of it adds to the teaching.
  • 🖼️ The pictures are the flyer’s. The eagle, the condor, the medallions, and the portraits all come from the flyer itself, and each page shows its flyer page as printed.
  • 🌿 The vine is the foundation’s. Across these sites, A Course in Miracles is the sky-blue strand and the plant medicine traditions the gold one, twining up into one point of light.
