🌄 Where it sits on the flyer

The Condor is the middle passage on page two, The Meeting of the Eagle and the Condor, between 🦅 The Eagle and 💛 Their Union. The page opens with the two streams that Plants & Miracles brings together:

One stream descends through the heart of Christian mysticism; the other rises through the living intelligence of sacred plants.

🐍 Why the serpent

The 🐍 beside the Condor, here and on the foundation’s home page, comes from the passage’s last words: “to teach the Ancient Caduceus.” A caduceus is the staff with two serpents twined around it. The flyer gives the Ancient Caduceus a page of its own: ✨ The Path of Awakening.

🌿 The gold strand

Across the foundation’s sites, the Condor is drawn as the gold strand of the vine: the strand for the plant medicine traditions. It twines upward with the Eagle’s sky blue until the two become one.
