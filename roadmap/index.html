🧭 Roadmap

TL;DR: The Holy Earth Foundation's GitHub home keeps Kenney's Teachings and an open record of the work on its sites. Next comes Kenney's welcome, a shared space for the community, a way to give to the foundation itself, and giving from a crypto wallet. Further out, a network of our own. No dates: each step lands in the changelog when it's ready.

Where the foundation’s GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What’s already done is in the changelog, and what keeps it all running is on the sustainability page. This is also the repository’s ROADMAP.md.

✅ Just landed

  • A full navbar on every page, with Teachings always in reach, folding into a Menu on smaller screens, with an icon on every link.
  • One window for every way to reach Kenney and the team, and one for the community: coming soon, with a sneak peek further out.
  • A map of every place these pages lead, Kenney’s Teachings and both sites included, each with its own picture.
  • A donate page that points to David A’s own Support David page, with a thank-you list for those who wish to be named.

🔜 Next

  • Kenney’s welcome. The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from Kenney, empty on purpose until he writes them.
  • A shared space for the community: a WhatsApp group and letters on Substack, both marked “coming soon” until they open.
  • A way to give to the foundation itself, and the foundation’s own words about gifts, so the placeholders on the donate page can go.
  • Give from a crypto wallet on the donate page: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets and email sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose until it’s ready.
  • A contacts page (CONTACTS.md) for this organization, so the placeholder in the Reach out window can go.

🌌 Later, and still being explored

  • A network of our own. A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where the community proposes ideas and decides together. Privacy first. A direction, not a promise.
