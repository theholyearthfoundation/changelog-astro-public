Where the foundation's GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What's already done is in the
[changelog](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog), and what keeps
it all running is on the
[sustainability](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page.
This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **A full navbar on every page**, with Teachings always in reach, folding into a Menu on smaller
  screens, with an icon on every link.
- **One window for every way to reach Kenney and the team**, and one for the community: coming
  soon, with a sneak peek further out.
- **A [map](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/map)** of every place these
  pages lead, Kenney's Teachings and both sites included, each with its own picture.
- **A [donate page](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/donate/)** that points to David A's own
  Support David page, with a thank-you list for those who wish to be named.

## 🔜 Next

- **Kenney's welcome.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from Kenney, empty on purpose
  until he writes them.
- **A shared space for the community:** a WhatsApp group and letters on Substack, both marked
  "coming soon" until they open.
- **A way to give to the foundation itself**, and the foundation's own words about gifts, so the
  placeholders on the donate page can go.
- **Give from a crypto wallet** on the donate page: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets
  and email sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose until it's ready.
- **A contacts page** (`CONTACTS.md`) for this organization, so the placeholder in the Reach out
  window can go.

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A network of our own.** A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like
  Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where the community proposes ideas and decides together.
  Privacy first. A direction, not a promise.
