📞 Reach Kenney directly

Reach out, on every page, used to send you to I Am One Self’s contact page for a number. Now the window shows Kenney’s own ways in, the same ones iamoneself.com publishes:

  • WhatsApp, the way Kenney keeps in touch day to day (“Contact me on WhatsApp or Email”).
  • +1 856-979-2316, for a call or a text.
  • Telegram: @iamoneself, Kenney’s channel, I am One Self - Daily Thoughts.

The email address, Kenney’s About the Speaker page, the free discovery call and the retreat application are all still there.

🤝 A pledge of support

The new pledge page is a way to say the foundation’s work matters to you. You read a short pledge, connect a crypto wallet (a browser wallet, a phone wallet by QR code, or an email sign-in) and sign it. In the foundation’s words:

I support The Holy Earth Foundation and the work it holds: Plants & Miracles, the retreats and coaching of I Am One Self, and David A’s visionary art and Chaiconi Bari teaching. Giving is receiving, and I offer my support in that spirit.

Signing is a message, not a transaction: there’s no gas, no money and no contract. The signature is checked in your browser and then forgotten; nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is recorded, and the page never shows your wallet’s address. There are no counts either: a signature proves a wallet signed, not that a unique person did, so no number will appear until pledges can be published as records anyone can check. Below the pledge, a small tool checks any pledge’s signature, right in the browser. A card marked “coming” describes gratitude tokens: a possible thank-you for anyone who’d like a deeper commitment, which can’t be sold or traded. No dates.

The pledge is in Get involved, in the navbar and the footer, and on the map.

🌿 Names, and David

Kenney is now credited as Isaiah (Kenney) across these pages: the people list, the welcome guide, the roles, the entries and the ledger. The teaching pages keep his flyer’s own words, exactly as printed.

David A’s call for help was a call for love. He shared himself with a brother, Kenney, and Spirit turned it into a way forward that blesses others. That’s how we came to the foundation’s work: a giving and receiving that began as a mustard seed, and is growing into a large tree. The portal, the people list and our earlier entry now tell it that way.

🖼️ Two cards, both kept

The foundation’s twin-vine card stays the portal’s. The porch now shows the illustrated card the portal first shipped with: the earth in a lattice of light, held by a seedling, over a painter’s studio full of winged hearts. On the map, the two front doors finally look different.

🧭 Smaller things

  • Donate and the welcome guide are always there: in the footer, in the navbar’s More menu and in the Menu, on every page.
  • The bottom strip says it scrolls. On a phone, a soft fade and a small arrow show there’s more to the side.
  • Receipts and taxes on the donate page: ask us for a receipt; whether a gift is tax-deductible depends on the organization’s status and your own situation. The foundation hasn’t published a status, so the page claims none.
  • These pages are planning to outlive any one person. The sustainability page and the roadmap now say who holds the keys today (Christopher W), and the steps to hand them to the foundation’s own people. I Am One Self’s and David A’s sites are being moved into the foundation’s organization, and Kenney will start working on them himself, with Codex.

Shared in WhatsApp, iMessage or Telegram, it arrives with its own card, drawn with the foundation's twin vine.
