Kenney's teaching moves into Teachings, a room of its own

TL;DR: Kenney's Plants & Miracles pages now live in their own section, Teachings, instead of the technical library. Every link that was already shared still works, the short link still previews as the flyer's cover, and not a word of his teaching changed.
Read the full story →

🌸 Why it moved

  • The library is for technical write-ups: how the foundation’s sites are built, what broke, and how it was fixed. Kenney’s teaching isn’t that, and it deserved better than sitting between a search-bug write-up and a browser quirk.
  • So it has a room of its own: Teachings. Today that’s Plants & Miracles, Kenney’s flyer taken apart into six pages: the whole flyer and where to begin, the Eagle, the Condor, their union, the Path of Awakening, and Kenney himself.

🔗 Nothing that was shared breaks

  • Every old address under the library still works and forwards straight to the same page in Teachings, carrying that page’s preview.
  • The short link, theholyearthfoundation.github.io/plants-and-miracles, now opens the Teachings hub, and still previews as the flyer’s own cover.

💬 His words stay his

  • Every passage is still quoted exactly as printed. Only the links between the pages changed.
  • The teaching is © Isaiah Kenney and shared with his permission; it stays outside this site’s own licenses, and the license note now names its new home.

Shared in WhatsApp, iMessage or Telegram, it arrives with its own card, drawn with the foundation's twin vine.
