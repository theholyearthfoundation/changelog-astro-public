Donations are real wallet connections — never a placeholder address

TL;DR: The donate flow uses a genuine wallet connection (not a fake demo address shown before the real one exists), and copy-to-clipboard for anyone who'd rather send directly.
Read the full story →

What changed

  • Donations connect through a real window.ethereum wallet integration — no placeholder or demo address is ever shown standing in for a real one.
  • A copy-to-clipboard option exists for anyone who prefers to send a transaction directly rather than connecting a wallet through the site.

Why it mattered

It would have been faster to ship a fake-looking donation address early and swap it in later, but that’s exactly the kind of shortcut that erodes trust in a space that already has enough of it. The deliberate policy here: never show something that looks like a real destination for funds until it actually is one.

Shared in WhatsApp, iMessage or Telegram, it arrives with its own card, drawn with the foundation's twin vine.
